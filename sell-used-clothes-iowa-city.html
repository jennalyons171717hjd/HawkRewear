Sell used clothes in Iowa City

Turn the pile on your closet floor into cash from people who live a few blocks away. Listing takes about five minutes, and you never need a shipping label.

See how handoffs work

List in four steps

1. Take photos

Use daylight and a plain wall. Show the front, the back, the tag, and any flaws.

2. Describe the piece

Add size, brand, condition, and era. Vintage pieces sell faster with a year or decade.

3. Set a starting bid

Pick a low start to attract bidders, or choose a fixed price. Most tees start between $8 and $15.

4. Hand it off

Meet the buyer, use a drop-off point, or ship it if you both agree.

What you keep

HawkRewear takes a 6% fee only when your item sells. Because most handoffs are local, you also skip the delivery fees that cut into your earnings on national resale apps.
