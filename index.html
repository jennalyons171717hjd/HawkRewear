Iowa City's closet, passed along.

HawkRewear is a local marketplace for secondhand and vintage clothing. Sell what you no longer wear, bid on pieces nobody else has, and hand things off in person so nobody pays for shipping.

Why Iowa City shops here

Local only

Every seller lives in Iowa City, so you can walk to a pickup, drive to a drop-off, or ask for shipping when that is easier.

Bid or buy now

Sellers choose a starting bid or a fixed price. Bids stay open for 72 hours, and you see the current bid at all times.

Less waste

Each jacket, dress, or sweater that stays in the closet loop is one fewer new garment made, and a better deal for you.
